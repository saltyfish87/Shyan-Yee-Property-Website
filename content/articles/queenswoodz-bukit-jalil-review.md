# Quick verdict: who should buy The Queenswoodz?

**My view.** Queenswoodz is the grown-up version of what Exsim is building next door at The Kingswoodz. There is no studio and no one-bedroom here: the smallest unit is 807 sq ft with two bedrooms plus a utility room, and the range runs to 1,410 sq ft. That single decision changes who lives in the building, and in my experience it also changes how a development ages. The station is a real walk — I measured 550 m to Awan Besar — and holding cost is about RM 420 a month on 1,000 sq ft across two GreenRE-certified towers on 3.44 acres. The detail I would point a buyer to is the floor plan of the tower itself: the upper floors taper from about 14 units down to 8, and then to just 4 on the top stretch. Ask for the stacking plan, not only the unit layout — on those upper floors you are buying a much quieter part of a 1,004-unit building.

- **Suits:** families and couples who want a 2 + 1 or larger home in Bukit Jalil; buyers who want the low-floor-count upper stacks; people who considered Kingswoodz but need more room.
- **Think twice if:** you want the cheapest entry price in the township, or you want freehold.

# Where is The Queenswoodz, and what is nearby?

In Bukit Jalil, the township built around the National Stadium and KL Sports City. The area is served by the Awan Besar, Alam Sutera, Muhibbah and Sri Petaling LRT stations, and by the Bukit Jalil Highway, KESAS, MEX, NPE and the Sungai Besi Expressway.

The shopping, schools and healthcare in the township are the same set that serves the other launches here: Pavilion Bukit Jalil and Aurora Place for retail, SJK(C) Lai Meng, Tzu Chi International School and Kingsgate International School for schooling, International Medical University and APU for campuses, and the 80-acre Bukit Jalil Recreational Park and Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort for recreation. Ask the sales team for the exact walking and driving distances from this site, as they differ from the Kingswoodz numbers.

The project page also lists 1,008 sq ft and 1,209 sq ft variants, so ask for the current type chart before choosing.

**What I would pick.** B1 or B2 at 936 sq ft is the layout I would put a young family in: three real bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a price that still sits under the freehold options nearby. A1 at 807 sq ft is the efficient choice for a couple, with the extra room as a study. If the household has parents or adult children living in, the 1,410 sq ft D1 dual key gives them a separate entrance. Whichever type you choose, ask which floors fall in the 8-unit and 4-unit bands.
